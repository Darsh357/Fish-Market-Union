Our Demands

Five things we're done asking nicely for.

Tap a card to see why it matters. These aren't luxuries — they're the bare minimum.

DEMAND NO. 01
⏱️

40-Min Breaks & Class Reset Buffers

Real breaks between classes to breathe, refuel, and reset — not a five-minute sprint down the hallway.

WHY IT MATTERS

The brain needs downtime to consolidate what it just learned. Back-to-back periods with zero buffer just trade attention for exhaustion — and everyone pays for it by 6th period.

DEMAND NO. 02
🌡️

Climate Protection & Classroom Cooling

Working fans, proper ventilation, and real heat-safety protocols. Nobody learns calculus at 40°C.

WHY IT MATTERS

Heat stress measurably tanks concentration and memory. Asking students to sit exams in a furnace isn't rigor, it's negligence.

DEMAND NO. 03
🔒

Anti-Paper Leak Security & Transparency

Tamper-proof exam handling and transparent grading. No more surprise retests because someone's paper leaked.

WHY IT MATTERS

Every leak punishes honest students twice — once with a rushed re-exam, and once with the message that the system protecting their work can't be trusted.

DEMAND NO. 04
📚

Daily Homework Time Caps

A hard ceiling on daily homework load — because evenings and weekends are supposed to exist too.

WHY IT MATTERS

Run the numbers in the calculator below — stacked homework and coaching hours are usually where "free time" quietly goes to die.

DEMAND NO. 05
🚫

End Demeaning Micro-management

Trust over surveillance. No public shaming, no ruler-measuring uniforms, no policing every small thing.

WHY IT MATTERS

Treating teenagers like suspects doesn't build discipline, it builds resentment. Respect is a two-way street, even in a classroom.
